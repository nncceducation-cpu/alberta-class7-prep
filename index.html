About the real Alberta Class 7 knowledge test

  • 30 multiple-choice questions — you need at least 25 correct (83%) to pass.
  • Taken at a registry agent office; a fee applies each attempt, and you can retake once per day.
  • You must be 14 or older (parent/guardian consent required if under 18).
  • Everything here is based on the official Alberta Driver's Guide — verify any rule at Alberta.ca.
